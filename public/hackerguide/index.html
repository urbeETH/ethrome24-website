How to reach Urbe Hub

All roads lead to Testaccio.

Metro, bus, tram and train

Take Metro B to Piramide, then walk 10 to 12 minutes. Roma Ostiense is the closest major train station.

  • Metro B: Piramide
  • Train: Roma Ostiense
  • Useful lines nearby: 170, 75, 23, 3
  • From Trastevere: cross Ponte Sublicio on foot
  • By car: free parking inside Largo Dino Frisullo
From Fiumicino

Best routes: FL1 regional train to Roma Ostiense, or Leonardo Express to Termini and Metro B to Piramide.

  • Leonardo Express to Termini: 32 minutes, EUR 14
  • Taxi to Urbe Hub: around EUR 60
From Ciampino

Take an airport bus to Termini, then Metro B to Piramide. Taxi is simpler if you arrive late.

  • Airport bus to Termini, then Metro B
  • ATAC bus 520 or 720 for public transport routes
  • Taxi to Urbe Hub: around EUR 50
Public transport basics

A BIT ticket costs EUR 1.50 and lasts 100 minutes across bus, tram and metro.

  • On metro: one journey, line changes allowed without exiting
  • Tap&Go contactless works on ATAC
  • Use Moovit or Roma Mobilita for routes

Food Manual

Roman food protocol.

A tiny survival guide for eating in Rome without accidentally starting a diplomatic incident at dinner.

Do not cut spaghetti.

Twirl it. If it looks chaotic, you are doing research.

Bread has a job.

Use it for the sauce left on the plate. This is not waste, this is closure.

Pineapple is a threat model.

On pizza, not today hacker. Stick to the classics.

Seafood and Parmesan: no.

If the pasta comes from the sea, the cheese stays away.

Cappuccino has office hours.

Morning only. After lunch, espresso is the protocol.

No ketchup on pasta.

Rome will not yell at you. Rome will silently remember.

Aperitivo is social infrastructure.

One drink, small bites, light conversation before dinner.

Do not rush dinner.

Italian meals are not a speedrun. Plan time for them.

Espresso is short.

If you want a longer coffee, ask for caffe lungo.

Gelato is always valid.
