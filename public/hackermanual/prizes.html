Prizes and Bounties

Two separate things are in play: the ETHRome prize and the sponsor bounties. They work under completely different rules.

The ETHRome prize

What the winning team takes home

One prize, one winning team. Awarded by the ETHRome judges.

ETHRome prize
One year at Urbe Hub, for the whole team

One annual membership for each member of the winning team, up to 3 people, at Rome's first web3 coworking space: the same room where the hackathon happens. One single ranking and one winning team, whatever you decided to build.

A year of desks, rooms and community in the building you are hacking in. How the winner is picked is on the Judging page.

Sponsor bounties

A different game, different referees

Sponsors put up their own bounties, on top of the ETHRome prize.

🗒️

How bounties work. Bounty winners are chosen exclusively by the sponsors. ETHRome has no say in the selection and does not handle the delivery of bounty prizes in any way. Each sponsor has its own brief, its own requirements and its own internal judging.
